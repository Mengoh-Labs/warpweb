# Asset provenance

`Sources/warpweb/Resources/kairo.webp` is the Kairo character artwork supplied by Quandry Labs and reused unchanged for WarpWeb. It is bundled locally; no external artwork service runs in the app. Its redistribution terms are in [LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md).

The executable uses Apple system frameworks and zlib provided by macOS, with no third-party package dependencies. Optional patches to the Warp terminal are not part of this distribution.
