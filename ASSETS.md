# Asset provenance

`Sources/warpweb/Resources/kairo.webp` is the Kairo character artwork supplied by Quandry Labs and reused unchanged for WarpWeb. It is bundled locally; no external artwork service runs in the app. It is licensed under **CC BY 4.0**, allowing reuse and adaptation with attribution to Quandry Labs. Include the license link and indicate changes; see [LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md) for the attribution and licensing details. Trademark rights remain separate.

The executable uses Apple system frameworks and zlib provided by macOS, with no third-party package dependencies. Optional patches to the Warp terminal are not part of this distribution.
